Ngiyathokoza ukubika ukuthi iBhange elikhulu laseNingizimu Afrika liphinde laba
nempumelelo enkulu kulonyaka odlulile. Lempumelelo ibe kuyoyonke imikhakha yeBhange.
Ngaphezu kwezinguquko ezisheshayo nezenza ukuthi kubenzima ukubona ukuthi isimo
somnotho womhlaba sizobanjani, sikwazile ukugcina saze sedlulela ukwehlisa amazinga
okwehla kwamandla emali. Lokhu sekwenze ukuthi isimo somnotho waseNingizimu
Afrika siqine futhi sikhule ngamandla.
Uhlelo lokuphathwa kwezimali olusimeme noluphathwa ngomthetho ofanele luyi
ngxenye ebaluleke kakhulu ekwenzeni ukuthi isimo sezomnotho sibe nempumelelo.
Umkhakha wokuphathwa kwezimali ubusagcinwe kahle, lokhu kubandakanya
nenqubekela phambili yamalungiselelo okuqalisa umthetho woku-phathwa
kwamabhange owaziwa ngokuthi yi Basel II. Okunye okube yimpumelelo kube
yizinguquko zokubolekisa ngezimali emabhange kanye nokungeniswa kwemali entsha
engamaphepha nemali yohlamvu engu 5 randi omusha.
Enye yezinkomba zempumelelo kube ukusimama kokungena kwezimali zangaphandle
ezitshalwa kuleli, osekwenze ukuthi likhule izinga lezimali ezilondolozwa emazweni
angaphandle. Lokhu sekudale ukuthi isimo sokushintshisana kwemali yaleli nezimali
zakwamanye amazwe sisimame.
Igxathu elikhulu kwafinyelelwa kulo ngo Disemba 2004 ngesikhathi irandi lamukelwa
njengenye yezimali eziyi 15 ezaziwayo umhlaba jikelele, ezihwebelana ohlelweni
olwaziwa ngokuthi yiContinuous Linked Settlement (CLS).

Intuthuko kwezomnotho wasekhaya
Umnotho wase Ningizimu Afrika ubulokhu ukhule njalo kusukela ngenyanga ka Septhemba
1999, okuyisikhathi eside esake saqoshwa emlandweni wakuleli wokukhula komnotho.
Esigabeni sesithathu sonyaka ka 2004, ukukhula komnotho kwafinyelela kumaphesenti
ama 5,7 ngonyaka. Kuthe engxenyeni yokuqala yonyaka ka 2005, umnotho wehla waya
kumaphesenti ama 3,9. Lokhu kukhula okungatheni kwabangelwa, ikakhulu, isimo
sangaphandle kwezwe esingesihle kanye nokukhula kwamandla erandi, okwaphinde
kwavimba ukuqhudelana kuleyo mikhakha yezomnotho ekhiqiza izimpahla.
Amathuba emisebenzi, ngaphandle komkhakha wezolimo, asalela emuva kunamazinga
omkhiqizo wasekhaya owabika ukukhula ngokulandelana kuleyo naleyo ngxenye
kwezinhlanu zonyaka kuya enyangeni kaSeptemba 2004. Kodwa-ke lobubungcono
abukwazanga ukuthi buqhubekele ezigabeni ezimbili zonyaka ezalandela.
Kuleminyaka emibili edlule amaholo abasebenzi akhula. Uma kuhlanganiswa ubungcono
bamathuba emisebenzi kanye nokukhula kwamaholo, loku kukhula kwenza ngcono
imali etholwa yimindeni. Ngalesosizathu, imali esetshenziswa yimindeni yakhula
ngamaphesenti ayi 6,1 ngonyaka ka 2004 kanye namaphesenti ayisi 6,0 ngonyaka,
engxenyeni yokuqala yonyaka ka 2005.
Imali esetshenziswe uhulumeni itshengise amazinga okukhula ngamaphesenti ayisi 7,2
ngonyaka ka 2004 kanye nangamaphesenti ama 5,3 engxenyeni yokuqala yonyaka ka
2005, lokhu okwalekelela ukuthi umthamo wemali esetshenziswa ekhaya ukhule.

Umgomo wokubolekisa ngezimali
Emhlanganweni odlule wabanikazi bamasheya, kwangithokozisa ukubika ukuthi
amazinga amanani okuthenga kwakulokhu aphakathi kwemigomo eyayibekiwe
engamaphesenti ama 3 kuya kwa 6 kusukela ngoSeptemba 2003. Onyakeni odlule,
lokhu kusebenza kahle kusemazingeni aphansi futhi akuzange kube nezinguquko
ezitheni. Kusukela ngo Julayi 2004, izinga lokwehla kwamandla emali (uma
singayifakanga inzalo-mboleko yokuthenga izindlu nezinye izakhiwo) uma kuqhathaniswa
ezinyangeni eziyishumi nambili, libe semazingeni angamaphesenti ama 3,9.
Ngo Januwari kulonyaka, amandla amanani ezinto zosizo ehla aba ngaphansi
kwamaphesenti ayisi 6 ngokokuqala selokhu kwasungulwa amazinga ahlosiwe okwehla
kwamandla emali. Okwathelela kakhulu lokhu kwehla kwamazinga amandla amanani
kwabangelwa imithetho yamanani asingethwe uhulumeni. Kusukela ngo Julayi 2004
ukwehla kwamandla amanani omkhiqizo nawo aqhubekile esemazingeni aphansi,
elinganiselwa phakathi kwe phesenti eli 1,7.
Lentuthuko enhle nokwenza ngcono ukwehla kwamandla emali, kwenza ukuthi
kuxegiswe inkambiso yokubolekisa ngezimali. Izinga lenzalo yokubolekisa ngezimali
lehliswa ngamaphuzu angama 50 ngo Agasti 2004 kanye nango Apreli 2005, kwaya
kumaphesenti ayi 7 ngonyaka.
Intuthuko ebonakalayo kulonyaka, kube yinguquko ebalulekile emazingeni okwehla
kwamandla emali. Ukuphathwa kahle komgomo wezokubolekisa ngezimali uxhumeke
ekutheni lomgomo uphathwa ngendlela efanele futhi ube nomthelela wokuthi ukwehla
kwamandla emali kuzoba kuliphi izinga ngokuzayo. Umphumela wenhlolovo eyenziwa
uMnyango woCwaningo kweZomnotho weNyuvesi yaseStellenbosch, ukhombise
ukwehla kwamandla emali ephakathi kwemigomo ebilindelekile kulonyaka odlule.
Kwingxenye yesigaba sokuqala sonyaka ka 2005, kanye neminyaka emithathu edlule,
ukwehla kwamandla emali kube ngaphakathi kwamazinga ahlosiwe kuyo yonke
imikhakha ebekiwe.
Amanye amaphuzu amahle ayinsika yokwenza ngcono isimo sokwehla kwamandla

Indlela okuphethwe ngayo ezezimali
Kwathi ngenyanga kaMeyi 2005, iBhange lasungula indlela entsha yokubolekisa
ngezimali. Injongo enkulu yalezi zinguquko kwaba ukuthi iBhange linciphise
ukuzibandakanya ekuhwebelaneni kwamabhange ngezimali liphinde lithuthukise

ngamabhange.

futhi kuphinde kube yindlela engenamfihlo. Lendlela izokwenza ukuthi kubengcono

amabhange angene kalula kuloluhlelo olusha. Loluhlelo luphinde lukhuthaze amabhange
ukuthi athathe iqhaza ekuphatheni izimali zawo ngokuzimele.

awo agcinwe yiBhange.

Imithetho yokusebenza kwamabhange
Kusukela ngomhlaka 1 ngenyanga ka Januwari 2008, amabhange aseNingizimu Afrika
angeke asakwazi ukusebenzisa isivumelwano esikhona mayelana nemithetho

ezobakhona maqondana nokuphathwa kwamabhange (Basel II) iyothunyelwa
emabhange isibandakanya nezindlela eyosebenza ngazo. IBhange liyathemba ukuthi
kungenzeka ukuthi noma yiliphi ibhange elibhaliswe eNingizimu Afrika likwazi
ukushinthsha lisebenzise indlela ye Basel II ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka okukhulu.

afake izicelo ezisemthethweni. Lezo zicelo ziyocubungulwa sokubonwa nanokuthi

zizokwenza ukuthi amabhange ancintisane futhi agqugquzele ukuthi usizo

bemithetho eqondene nokuphathwa kwazo zonke lezi zikhungo.
