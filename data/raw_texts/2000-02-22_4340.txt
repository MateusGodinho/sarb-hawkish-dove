Titulo: 2000-02-22: Postponement of the Monetary Policy Committee Meeting
Data da reuniao: 2000-02-22
URL: https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2000/4340
Fonte do texto: html
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Due to the rescheduling of the date for the Minister of Finance's annual Budget Speech from March 2000 to Wednesday, 23 February 2000, the Reserve Bank has postponed the date of its next Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meeting. The meeting moves from 23 and 24 February 2000 to 1 and 2 March 2000. The dates for the other MPC meetings for the rest of 2000 remain as scheduled: 5 and 6 April 18 and 19 May 14 and 15 June 10 and 11 August 20 and 21 September 15 and 16 November. Please note that all the dates given serve as a guideline only and are therefore subject to change. Also note that all MPC statements are released only at the end of each meeting, that is, on the second day.
